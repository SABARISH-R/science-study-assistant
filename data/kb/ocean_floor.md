# Ocean Floor

## Main features of the ocean floor
The ocean floor has continental shelves near land, a steeper continental slope, a flat abyssal plain, mid-ocean ridges, and deep ocean trenches. Most of the deep seafloor is a vast, flat abyssal plain covered in fine sediment.

## Mid-ocean ridges and seafloor spreading
Mid-ocean ridges are underwater mountain chains where two plates diverge. Magma rises, cools and forms new oceanic crust, so the seafloor spreads outward. Crust is youngest at the ridge and gets older with distance from it.

## Ocean trenches
Ocean trenches are the deepest parts of the ocean. They form at subduction zones where a denser oceanic plate bends and sinks beneath another plate. The Mariana Trench in the western Pacific contains the deepest known point on Earth.

## How scientists map the seafloor
Ships use sonar, which sends sound pulses and times the echoes to measure depth. Satellites measure tiny changes in sea-surface height that reflect seafloor shape, and submersibles allow direct observation.
