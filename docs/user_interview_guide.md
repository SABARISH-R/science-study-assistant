# User interview guide

Use this for 30-minute conversations. Record findings in `docs/interview_notes.md` (create it after the first interview). Do not invent or pre-fill answers; record only what people actually say.

## Intro (2 min)
Explain that you are researching how students get help with science after class, that there are no right answers, and ask permission to take notes.

## Teachers
1. Walk me through the last time students asked you a question after the lesson ended.
2. Where do your students go for answers outside class? What have you seen go wrong?
3. How do you feel about students using AI chatbots for science? What would make you trust one?
4. If a tool only answered from notes you approved, what would you want it to do when it did not know?
5. Which topics generate the most follow-up questions?

## Students
1. Tell me about the last time you got stuck on a science topic. What did you do?
2. What do you use to find answers (videos, chatbots, friends, textbooks)? What do you like and dislike about each?
3. How do you know when an answer is right?
4. Show me how you would ask this tool a question. What did you expect to happen?
5. When it says "I don't know", what would you want next?
